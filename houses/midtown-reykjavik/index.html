About Midtown ReykjavikAboutMidtownReykjavik

Midtown Reykjavik is a self check-in property offering a variety of modern and bright hotel rooms.

All of our guests can experience a comfortable stay in the heart of Reykjavik. Choose from a selection of stylish standard double / twin rooms or fabulous suites on the top floor to complement your trip to Iceland.

Located in the heart of downtown, the hotel is surrounded by lively bars, restaurants, and nightly live music until 11pm from the bar in our building. There may be some nighttime noise, but it’s all part of Reykjavik’s vibrant atmosphere—perfect for those looking to join in the fun!

Please note that this is a self check-in property.
