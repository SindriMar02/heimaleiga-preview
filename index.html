HistoryHistory

2014

The Melax family starts renting out family members' homes and sees the opportunity in contactless self check-in.

2017

Heimaleiga is officially founded. By the end of the year the first multi-apartment property, Icelandic Comfort Apartments, joins and Heimaleiga services 70 apartments.

2019

Sif Apartments, a large serviced apartment complex created from scratch: Heimaleiga is involved from the sale of the apartments to investors, through design, to getting them ready for guests.

2020

Blue Mountain Apartments and AVA Apartments are taken on. Heimaleiga partners with Guesty, software made for short-term rental. From 2020 every new project is fully licensed, owned by professional investors and rented all year round.

2022

Cleaning and maintenance are fully in-house, and a professional laundry opens next to the Icelandic office at Grensásvegur 14. In 2022 Heimaleiga cleans 11,592 apartments.

2023

Icelandic Apartments and Swan House open. The first hotel project, Room With a View, is rebranded: the apartments become Máni Apartments and the hotel rooms become Midtown Hotel.

2024

Frost Apartments and B2 Apartments open, both fully renovated. In peak season up to 80 people work at Heimaleiga.

2025

Aurora Nooks and EIR Apartments open from the ground up. Aurora Nooks has its own underground parking and EIR Apartments sleep up to 10 people in four bedroom apartments.
